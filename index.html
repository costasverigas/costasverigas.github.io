About

Sound is intentional.

I'm a mixing and mastering engineer and producer based in Athens, with over 15 years of studio experience across a wide range of genres. My credits span 300+ songs — from intimate singer-songwriter records to full-band productions.

I work closely with artists from tracking through to final master, treating every session as a collaboration. The goal is never to impose a sound — it's to find the one the song is already asking for, and deliver it with clarity and depth.

My work is independently tracked and verified on Muso.AI, currently ranking in the top 2% of mastering engineers and top 5% of mixing engineers worldwide.

Also on the road: Alongside studio work, I bring 15+ years of live sound and FOH engineering experience — including major festivals, theatre productions, and international touring productions across Greece and Switzerland.
